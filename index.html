About
Two friends who got tired of the apps.

By Design started over a long dinner and a longer list of grievances about modern dating. We thought the people we knew deserved better than another swipe — better questions, smaller rooms, and someone who'd actually thought about who they should meet.

So that's what we do now. Once a month, in Manhattan. Guest list capped tight, questionnaire read carefully. No algorithm, no randoms. Just intentional evenings for people who are genuinely ready.

We host curated matchmaking evenings for NYC professionals aged 25–35. Every guest is pre-matched before the doors open.
