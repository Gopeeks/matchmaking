Our Story

Intentional connection in a city that never slows down.

We're Gopika and Pratishka — two New Yorkers who believe the best relationships don't happen by accident. Kindred is our answer to a city full of remarkable single people who just haven't found the right room yet.

✦

Pre-Matched, Always

Before every event you complete a compatibility questionnaire. We do the matching — you show up ready to meet someone we've already chosen for you.

◈

Curated & Intimate

This isn't a mixer. Every guest is here for a reason. The energy shifts when everyone's been selected with intention — and you can feel it.

◉

NYC Professionals, 25–35

Our guests are ambitious, genuinely social, and ready to meet someone. Monthly events mean more chances, more connections, more stories.

The Process

Simple. Intentional. Exciting.

Three steps stand between you and the person you've been waiting to meet.

01
📋

Apply & Fill Out Your Profile

Submit your interest and complete our thoughtful compatibility questionnaire. Tell us who you are, what you value, and what you're genuinely looking for.

02
✨

We Do the Matching

Gopika and Pratishka personally review every response and pair each guest with a compatible match before the event. Human curation, not algorithms.

03
🥂

Show up to an intimate evening in NYC, enjoy the atmosphere, and meet the person we've chosen for you. The rest is up to both of you.
